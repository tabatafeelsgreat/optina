Prayer requests & candles

If you cannot come to church

Submit a prayer request and it will be read at the next service. Please list only names of those baptized in the Orthodox Church.

Commemoration at the Liturgy. At the Proskomedia the priest takes particles from the prosphora for the living and the departed, and at the end of the Liturgy places them in the Holy Chalice, praying that their sins be washed away.

Moleben is a service of supplication to the Lord, the Theotokos or the saints, or of thanksgiving for blessings received. Panikhida is a memorial service for the departed.

Candle. Our altar servers will light your candle at the next service: small $2, medium $4, large $7.

About the parish

Our history

The parish was founded in 1989. In 2007 it joined the Russian Orthodox Church Outside of Russia, Diocese of Eastern America. Ours is a multinational community: people from Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Moldova, Bulgaria, Romania and American converts to Orthodoxy pray here together.

Services are held in the Saint Theodore's Chapel building on Granby Street in Norfolk.
